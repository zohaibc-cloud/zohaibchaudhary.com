IThe Sparkچنگاری

Every one of us is born with a spark.

It isn't ambition or a number on a scorecard. It's curiosity, imagination and resilience.

That little spark is hidden in all of us.

IIThe Edges of Growthسفر

Resilience. Movement. Belonging.

Lahore

My story runs from Lahore to Montreal to Toronto, and beyond.

Montréal

Urdu became French, then English, then the whole world.

Cultures collided. I adapted.

Toronto

Growth lives at the edges. Every transition left a mark.

We carry our roots, our flaws and our resilience, and they shape how we lead.

IIIThe Weight of Systemsنظام

I once chased medicine to heal people.

But I learned quickly that it's rarely the person who breaks. It's the system around them.

In business, healthcare, education and finance, I kept finding cracks no one person could fix alone.

Systems don't fix themselves. We have to reimagine them, together.

IVAnchored by Familyگھر

Family. Faith. Future.

The greatest work happens at home. Leadership starts at the dinner table, in the quiet moments, long before anyone gives you a title.

Every day, the three people who call me Baba remind me that leadership isn't scale first. It's presence first.

That's where the real legacy is built.

“Wherever you stand, be the soul of that place.”Rumi
VWhat Drives Meتجسس

Curiosity and connection.

I'm curious about people,

  • how they think,
  • what they hope for
  • and what holds them back.

And I believe that behind every system and every strategy, there's a person with a story.

Real change begins in the conversations most people rush past, in the questions that unlock what's been buried.
